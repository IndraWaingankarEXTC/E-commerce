Cultural Legacy of India

A Tribute to Indian Folk Arts & Animal Motifs

For thousands of years, Indian indigenous art traditions have honored biodiversity through natural dyes, geometric storytelling, and revered animal archetypes across diverse regions.

Bihar • Mithila

Madhubani Art

Tigers & Fish

Characterized by intricate double-line borders, floral fillers, and eye-catching natural pigments derived from turmeric, indigo, and soot. Animals such as fish symbolize fertility, while peacocks and tigers embody romance, majesty, and divine protection.

Key Colors: Turmeric yellow, deep indigo, lampblack, terracotta ochre.
Maharashtra • Sahyadri

Warli Tribal Art

Bulls & Horses

Created on mud-plastered hut walls using white rice paste, Warli art uses fundamental shapes—triangles, circles, and lines—to illustrate harmonious tribal life alongside animals, forest deer, and rhythmic ceremonial dances like the Tarpa.

Key Colors: Rice white on natural red clay or cow-dung wash backgrounds.
Rajasthan • Nathdwara

Pichwai Painting

Cows & Peacocks

Originated as intricate cloth hangings behind deities in Rajasthan temples. Known for gold leaf embellishments, lotus motifs, dancing peacocks herald of monsoon rains, and gentle cows adorned with ornamental headgears and bell necklaces.

Key Colors: Real gold leaf, mineral greens, vermilion, and ultramarine.
Odisha • Raghurajpur

Pattachitra Scrolls

Elephants & Swans

Painted on specially treated cloth canvas or palm leaves with conch shell white and stone powders. Celebrates temple elephants draped in embroidered tapestries alongside mythological lore, lush jungle flora, and geometric decorative borders.

Key Colors: Conch shell white, hingula red, haritala yellow, and leaf green.
Madhya Pradesh • Narmada Valley

Gond Art

Forest Deer & Birds

Drawn by the indigenous Gond tribe, this art uses meticulous dots and fine lines to give motion to animals, sacred mahua trees, tigers, and flying cranes, embodying the philosophy that seeing a sacred image brings auspicious fortune.

Key Colors: Colored soils, charcoal, plant saps, and vibrant mineral shades.
Andhra Pradesh • Srikalahasti

Kalamkari Craft

Lions & Deer

Hand-painted or block-printed cotton textiles created using sharp bamboo pens and natural vegetable dyes. Showcases graceful peacocks, majestic royal lions, and flowing creepers depicting classic regional epics and nature.

Key Colors: Pomegranate rind yellow, iron rust black, and madder root red.
